Meet Stephanie

Where technique
meets warmth.

Colombia is known around the world for body treatments: manual lymphatic drainage, maderoterapia (sculpting with wooden tools) and recovery care after cosmetic surgery. Stephanie learned these methods at home and brings them to her London clients.

Each session starts with a short conversation about what your body needs that day. Then she works with care, attention and the kind of hands you don't forget.
